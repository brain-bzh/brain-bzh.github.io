Mathieu Léonardon defended his *Habilitation à Diriger des Recherches* (HDR) on **Monday, May 4, 2026** at IMT Atlantique!

### Topic

**« Sobriety through Co-design in Deep Learning and Digital Communications »**

His research addresses algorithmic sobriety, hardware-software co-design, and energy-efficient implementations for deep learning models and digital communication systems.


### Jury Members

- **Steven Derrien** — Professor, Université de Bretagne Occidentale (Brest)
- **Catherine Dezan** — Associate Professor, Université de Bretagne Occidentale (Brest)
- **Bertrand Le Gal** — Associate Professor, Université de Rennes (Lannion)
- **Frédéric Pétrot** — Professor, Grenoble INP / Ensimag (Grenoble)
- **Andrea Pinna** — Professor, Sorbonne Université (Paris)
